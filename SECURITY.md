# Security Policy

We take the security of Athar and the privacy of its users seriously.

## Supported versions

Security fixes are applied to the latest released version of the web app and of the Android and iOS apps. Older versions are not patched; please update to the latest release.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately. Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion.

- Email: <amr@gharib.dev> with the subject `ATHAR security report`

Include, where possible:

- the affected platform and app version;
- a description of the issue and its impact;
- the steps or a proof of concept needed to reproduce it.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge reports within 3 business days.
- We give an initial assessment within 10 business days.
- We keep you informed until the issue is resolved and tell you when the fix is released.

## Guidelines for researchers

- Test only with accounts and data that belong to you.
- Do not access, change or delete other users' data.
- Do not run denial-of-service, spam or social-engineering attacks.
- Give us reasonable time to fix an issue before disclosing it publicly.

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who follow these guidelines and act in good faith.

## Scope

In scope: the Athar web app at `athark.org`, the Android and iOS apps, and the Athar backend services.

Out of scope: third-party services the app relies on (report those to their operators), and findings that require a rooted or jailbroken device or physical access to an unlocked device.
