Amid the rush of daily life, the soul needs a quiet place to rest. Athar is a calm companion for dhikr, Quran recitation and renewing your connection with Allah, built to help you form lasting habits without distractions.

THE QURAN IN YOUR HANDS
Read the Mushaf in an eye-friendly design, keep your place and your khatma plan, listen to recitations and Quran radio, read tafsir, and learn the Quran's vocabulary. If you memorise, practise recitation by hiding and revealing verses gradually.

DAILY ADHKAR
The authentic morning, evening, post-prayer and sleep adhkar with their virtue and count, progress tracking, your own custom adhkar, and favourites.

PRAYER
Prayer times with a countdown to the next prayer, Qibla direction, nearby mosques, and reminders you control.

TASBIH
A fast, floating digital tasbih that follows you around the app, with daily totals.

WIRD, MUHASABA AND QADAA
A wird table to follow your worship day by day, a muhasaba table to take yourself to account, and a qadaa section to track missed prayers and make them up step by step.

SUNAN AND DUAS WITH SOURCES
900+ sunan, each with its hadith, narrator and number, including forgotten sunnahs. Duas of the Prophets and the Companions, and duas for refuge, forgiveness and guidance.

ASK ATHAR
An AI companion for questions about the Quran, hadith and Islamic meanings, grounded in the sources available. It works without an account and needs an internet connection; your messages are sent to an AI service provider for processing.

ISLAMIC LIBRARY
Hadith books and selections with explanations, grading and difficult words; save hadith and practise memorising them. Stories of the Prophets, the Seerah, the Names of Allah, the pillars of Islam and faith, ruqyah, and guides to wudu and prayer.

EVERY DAY
Dua of the day, sunnah of the day, a verse, a hadith, a reflection and a Quran word, plus the Quran's scientific miracles, short clips and courses.

YOUR JOURNEY
Statistics, goals and a friendly leaderboard.

OFFLINE
Read the Quran, adhkar and anything you have already loaded without a connection. Recitations, sync, the AI companion and some services need the internet.

PURE AND PRIVATE
There are no ads inside Athar. We do not track your location in the background; with your permission it is used only for prayer times, Qibla and nearby mosques. An account is optional, and you can delete it and its data from inside the app at any time.

Every tasbih you whisper, every verse you recite, every prostration leaves a mark that is never erased. What mark will you leave today?
