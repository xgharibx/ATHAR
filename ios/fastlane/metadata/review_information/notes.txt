Athar is a free Arabic Islamic app (Quran reader, adhkar, prayer times, qibla, tasbih, hadith library, daily duas and sunan).

No account is needed to use any feature. Signing in is optional and only syncs progress between devices (email magic link; no password). The reviewer can use the whole app without signing in. Account deletion is in Settings > Account and sync > Delete account.

Permissions are all optional and requested in context:
- Location (when in use): prayer times, qibla direction, nearby mosques. Never tracked in the background.
- Notifications: prayer and adhkar reminders.
- Microphone / speech recognition: optional voice counting in the tasbih screen.
- Photos (add only): saving shared dhikr/verse images.

"Ask Athar" is an optional AI companion for Islamic questions. It needs internet; messages go to an AI service provider through our server. It works without an account. The privacy policy is at https://www.athark.org/privacy.html and is linked in the app.

The app has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.
