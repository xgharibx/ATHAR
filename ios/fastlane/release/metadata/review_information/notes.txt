Athar is a free Arabic Islamic app (Quran reader, adhkar, prayer times, qibla, tasbih, hadith library, daily duas and sunan).

The core app and Ask Athar can be used without an account. Signing in is optional and enables cloud sync and account-specific features (email magic link; no password). Account deletion is in Settings > Account and sync > Delete account. Reviewers can access the core app without signing in. Location permission is optional; a city can be selected manually for prayer times.

Permissions are all optional and requested in context:
- Location (when in use): prayer times, qibla direction, nearby mosques. Never tracked in the background.
- Notifications: prayer and adhkar reminders.
- Photos (add only): saving shared dhikr/verse images.

"Ask Athar" is an optional AI companion for Islamic questions. It needs internet; requests pass through Supabase to MiniMax. It works without an account. Prayer times can use Aladhan; nearby mosque search can use Overpass/OpenStreetMap. The privacy policy is at https://www.athark.org/privacy.html and is linked in the app.
On iOS, the first AI message requires the user's explicit approval before it is sent. Users can withdraw that approval in Settings; future messages remain unsent until they approve again.

Athar does not display its own ads, though third-party content providers may show ads within their content. The app has no third-party analytics or tracking.
