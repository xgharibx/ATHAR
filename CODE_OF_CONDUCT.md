# Code of Conduct

Athar serves people in their worship. Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, reviews or any other channel, is expected to act accordingly.

## Expected

- Treat others with respect and assume good intent.
- Keep discussion on the work: the behaviour of the app, the accuracy of its content, the quality of the code.
- Raise disagreement about religious content with sources, courteously, and accept that differences among recognised scholars exist.
- Protect privacy: do not post anyone's personal information, including in screenshots and logs.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, or demeaning remarks about any person or group.
- Sectarian argument or attacks on scholars or schools of thought.
- Spam, promotion, or content unrelated to the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to <amr@gharib.dev>. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation to keep the project respectful.
