## Why this policy exists

People rely on the Athar name to know that the Quran text, adhkar and other content they are reading come from Athar and have been checked by it. A copy, or a modified version, published under the same name would mislead them. Protecting the marks protects that trust.

## What you may do without asking

- Refer to Athar by name, in plain text, to identify the application truthfully: for example in a review, an article or a comparison.
- Link to athark.org or to the official store listings.
- Use an unmodified screenshot of the application to illustrate a review or article about it.

When you do, do not suggest that Athar sponsors, endorses or is affiliated with you.

## What you may not do

- use an Athar mark, or anything confusingly similar, as or in the name, icon or logo of any application, website, product, service, company, domain name or social media account;

## Asking for permission

Send requests to <amr@gharib.dev> with the subject `ATHAR trademark request`. Describe who you are, which mark you want to use, where and for how long. Permission is valid only when given in writing.

## Reporting misuse

If you find an application, listing, website or account that uses an Athar mark without permission, or that impersonates Athar, please report it to <amr@gharib.dev> with a link.
