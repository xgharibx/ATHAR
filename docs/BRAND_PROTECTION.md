# Brand Protection: Registering the Athar Marks

The licence and trademark policy in this repository state Athar's rights. Registration is what makes them straightforward to enforce: it gives the owner a certificate to show an app store, a host or a court. This guide lists what to register and where. It is a working checklist, not legal advice; confirm current procedure and fees with the office or with an Egyptian IP attorney before filing.

**Owner of record for every filing:** Amr Abdel-Aziz Gharib (or a company he later assigns the rights to; assignments must then be recorded with each office).

## 1. Trademarks

### What to file

| # | Mark | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ATHAR | Word mark, Latin script | The name as used in store listings and the domain |
| 2 | أثر | Word mark, Arabic script | The name users see in the app |
| 3 | The Athar logo | Figurative mark | The strongest of the three: a common word can be hard to register on its own, a distinctive drawing is not |

### Classes (Nice Classification)

| Class | Covers | Needed for |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | Downloadable mobile applications and software | The apps themselves: file first |
| 41 | Education and religious instruction | The learning and Quran content |
| 42 | Online, non-downloadable software | The web app and the assistant |

### Before filing: clearance search

"Athar / أثر" is an ordinary Arabic word, and other apps and companies use it. Search the Egyptian register and the app stores for identical or similar marks in the same classes. If the plain word is blocked or weak, file the logo and the word together with a distinctive element, and rely on the logo.

### Where

1. **Egypt first.** File with the Egyptian trademark office, now part of the Egyptian Intellectual Property Authority (EGIPA). Egypt registers to the first person to file, so file before promoting the app further. Expect roughly a year or more from filing to certificate. A registration can be cancelled if the mark is unused in Egypt for five consecutive years.
2. **Other countries through the Madrid System.** Egypt is a member. Once the Egyptian application exists, one international application through WIPO can extend it to the countries where Athar has users, such as Saudi Arabia, the UAE and other markets the Madrid System covers.

### After registration

- Use ® next to a mark only in countries where it is registered; use ™ elsewhere. Update `TRADEMARKS.md`, the website footers and the store listings accordingly.
- Diarise the renewal date.
- Use the certificate in app-store trademark complaints against copies and impersonators.

## 2. Copyright

Copyright in the code, the design and the logo exists from creation, without registration. A deposit gives dated proof of ownership, which helps in a dispute.

- **Software:** Egypt's Information Technology Industry Development Agency (ITIDA) runs the office for depositing computer programs and databases. Deposit the app's source as of a release.
- **Logo and artwork:** if anyone other than the owner designed the logo, icon or splash artwork, get a signed written assignment of all rights to the owner, and keep the original design files.
- **Evidence:** keep dated records of first use: the first store listing, the domain registration and early release builds.

## 3. Names and accounts to hold

- Domains: `athark.org` is held. Consider the obvious variants so they cannot be used to impersonate Athar.
- App store developer accounts, the GitHub organisation, and social media handles under the Athar name, all registered to the owner.

## 4. Open licence items that affect the brand

Unlicensed third-party material in the app is the easiest ground for a takedown against Athar itself. Resolve the items listed in [CONTENT_LICENSES.md](CONTENT_LICENSES.md) before a wider launch.
